AM&SON Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how AMSON ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information when you access or use the AMSON mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Platform").

By accessing or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the Platform.

1. Scope and Applicability

This Policy applies to personal information collected through:

  • The AMSON iOS and Android mobile applications;
  • User accounts, subscriptions, and in-app features;
  • Push notifications and communications;
  • Interactions with all Platform features.

This Policy does not apply to third-party websites, services, or applications that may be linked from the Platform.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

This may include:

  • Email address and account login credentials;
  • Subscription and billing information;
  • Stocks and public figures you add to your personal Watchlist;
  • Communications sent to us (e.g., support emails).

B. Information Collected Automatically

When you access or use the Platform, we may automatically collect:

  • Device information (device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers);
  • A push notification token used to deliver alerts to your device;
  • IP address and general location (e.g., country or region);
  • Usage data (features accessed, session duration, searches performed);
  • Log data and error reports.

C. Market and Public Filing Data

The Platform displays stock market data, signals, and public filing data obtained from third-party data providers. For security purposes, we do not publicly disclose the specific providers we use. We do not collect brokerage account credentials, real-money trading account information, or actual investment portfolio data.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your AMSON account;
  • Store and sync your Watchlist across devices;
  • Deliver personalized push notifications, including:
    • Signal change alerts (BUY, SELL, or HOLD) for stocks on your Watchlist;
    • Notable new trading, filing, or news activity for stocks on your Watchlist;
    • Activity changes for public figures you follow;
    • Reminders to build your Watchlist if it is empty;
  • Process subscription payments and manage access to AMSON Pro features;
  • Improve Platform functionality, performance, and user experience;
  • Monitor usage, prevent fraud, and enforce our Terms & Conditions;
  • Comply with legal obligations.
We do not use personal data to provide financial advice or personalized investment recommendations. All signals are generated algorithmically and are for informational purposes only.

4. Push Notifications

AMSON sends push notifications to your device via a push notification token stored in your user profile.

Types of push notifications we send include:

  • Signal change alerts (BUY, SELL, or HOLD) for stocks on your Watchlist;
  • Notable new trading, filing, or news activity alerts for stocks on your Watchlist;
  • Activity change alerts for public figures you follow;
  • A periodic reminder to build your Watchlist if it is empty.

You can disable push notifications at any time through your device's system settings. Disabling notifications does not affect access to any other Platform features.

5. Subscriptions and Payments

AMSON Pro subscriptions are billed through the Apple App Store (iOS) and Google Play Store (Android). We do not store your payment card information. All billing is handled directly by Apple or Google according to their respective privacy policies.

6. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We do not sell personal information.

A. Service Providers

We share information with third-party service providers who help us operate the Platform, including providers of account authentication, secure data storage, market and public filing data, subscription billing, and push notification delivery. For security reasons, we do not publicly name these providers. Each is authorized to use your information only as necessary to perform services on our behalf and is bound by its own privacy obligations.

B. Legal and Compliance Obligations

We may disclose information if required to:

  • Comply with applicable laws, regulations, or legal processes;
  • Respond to lawful requests from authorities;
  • Protect the rights, property, or safety of AMSON, users, or others;
  • Enforce our Terms & Conditions.

C. Business Transfers

If AMSON is involved in a merger, acquisition, asset sale, or similar transaction, user information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Your account data and Watchlist are stored using secure cloud infrastructure. We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Platform's services.

If you delete your account, we will take reasonable steps to delete your personal data from our systems within a reasonable timeframe, subject to any legal retention obligations.

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices, including authentication and encryption, to protect your personal information. Access to your Watchlist data is restricted to your authenticated account only — other users cannot view your data. For security reasons, we do not publicly disclose the specific technical measures or infrastructure we use.

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. You acknowledge and accept this inherent risk.

9. User Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access or correct personal data we hold about you;
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing;
  • Opt out of push notifications via your device settings;
  • Cancel your AMSON Pro subscription through the App Store or Google Play.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@amandson.com. We may require verification of your identity before fulfilling requests.

10. U.S. State Privacy Rights

If you are a resident of California, Virginia, Colorado, or other U.S. states with applicable privacy laws, you may have additional rights under those laws. AMSON does not sell personal data as defined by applicable U.S. state privacy laws.

Requests related to state privacy rights may be submitted to support@amandson.com.

11. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a minor, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

12. International Users

The Platform is operated from the United States. If you access the Platform from outside the U.S., you acknowledge that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States or other jurisdictions with different data protection laws.

13. Third-Party Services

The Platform relies on third-party service providers to operate. AMSON is not responsible for the privacy practices of any third party. For security reasons, we do not publicly list these providers; if you have questions about a specific category of data processing, contact us at support@amandson.com.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. If changes are material, we will provide reasonable notice through the Platform or by email. Continued use of the Platform after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

The most current version will always be available on the Platform.
